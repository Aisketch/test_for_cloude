## 📝 USAGE INSTRUCTIONS

1. **Start with 2-3 core languages** maximum at launch
2. **Prioritize professional translation** for critical content
3. **Build glossary early** to ensure consistency
4. **Test with native speakers** before launch
5. **Monitor language-specific metrics** separately
6. **Plan for ongoing translation costs**
7. **Document everything** for future language additions

---

## ⚠️ CRITICAL REMINDERS

- Professional translation for customer-facing content is worth the investment
- AI translation needs human review for quality
- Each language needs separate keyword research for SEO
- Cultural adaptation > literal translation
- Test layouts with longer languages (German expands ~30%)
- Maintain translation memory to reduce costs
- Budget for ongoing content updates across all languages
- Native speaker QA is essential before launch
- Don't assume all languages convert equally - test and optimize per market
