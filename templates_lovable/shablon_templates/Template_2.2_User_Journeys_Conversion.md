## 📝 USAGE INSTRUCTIONS

1. **Interview real users** or existing customers for accurate persona data
2. **Map actual behavior** not assumed behavior
3. **Identify friction points** through user testing
4. **Prioritize optimizations** by impact and effort
5. **Test hypotheses** with A/B testing
6. **Iterate based on data** not opinions
7. **Monitor metrics** continuously after launch

---

## ⚠️ CRITICAL REMINDERS

- User journeys should be based on real data when possible
- Don't assume - test your hypotheses
- Every page should have a clear next step
- Mobile users often have different journeys than desktop
- Conversion optimization is ongoing, not one-time
- Track everything - you can't optimize what you don't measure
- Remove friction at every opportunity
- Make CTAs clear, compelling, and visible
